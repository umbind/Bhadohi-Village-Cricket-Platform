# Privacy Policy for Bhadohi Cricket (भदोही क्रिकेट)

**Effective Date:** October 4, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 4, 2026  
**Application ID:** `org.bvcp.bhadohi_cricket`  
**Platform:** Bhadohi Village Cricket Platform (BVCP)

---

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Bhadohi Cricket** (भदोhi Village Cricket Platform), a self-service sports coordination application designed exclusively for adult cricket enthusiasts and tournament organizers across Bhadohi district (Sant Ravidas Nagar), Uttar Pradesh.

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Policy and Indian Information Technology laws.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
1. **Account Credentials:**
   - 10-digit mobile number (used strictly for authentication and account recovery).
   - 6-digit Secret PIN (stored securely using one-way cryptographic hashing).
   - 8-character district recovery code.
2. **Player Profile Details:**
   - Full Name, Home Village, Block name (Gyanpur, Aurai, Bhadohi, Suriyawan, Deegh, Abholi).
   - Cricket playing role, batting style, and bowling style.
   - Playing availability toggle (active 15-day rolling window).
3. **Team & Tournament Operational Data:**
   - Team name, registered tournament, squad member roster names.

---

## 3. How We Protect Your Privacy (Zero Invariants)
- **100% Phone Number Masking:** Mobile numbers are NEVER displayed publicly in the app. They are strictly masked as `98XXXXXX21` in all public search and team rosters.
- **No Financial / Payment Data:** We do NOT collect bank accounts, credit cards, UPI IDs, or digital wallet details. Entry fees for village tournaments are strictly offline notice ("Cash on Ground").
- **No Background Tracking or GPS:** The app does NOT track your background location.
- **Age Restriction (18+ Only):** The platform is strictly for adults (18 years or older). We do not collect information from minors.
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** The platform does not host commercial advertising networks or sell user data.

---

## 4. Permissions Used
- **Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Used to synchronize tournament schedules and squad details.
- **Access Network State (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Used to determine offline vs online status.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion
Users retain full control over their account. You can edit your profile details anytime or delete your profile directly from within the app settings.
